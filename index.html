Holiday with no name marks those dates as holidays; with a name it also marks that colleague off work that day.
Unavailable marks a colleague off work (whole day) on those dates — e.g. Andy: sep1,5 — and shows in the Unavailable column.
Manual shifts require a name and lock that person in for those dates. Morning runs weekdays, Deployment runs weekdays only, and Weekend Support covers Sat/Sun/public holidays — each is kept balanced across everyone. Manual locks are honored even on non-typical days.
